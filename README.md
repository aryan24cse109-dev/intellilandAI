# IntelliLandAI

> AI-powered Intelligent Land Record Digitization, Validation and Parcel Linking Platform

IntelliLandAI is a prototype platform designed to modernize land-record workflows by combining document AI, OCR, structured data extraction, validation, human verification and GIS-based parcel visualization.

The platform converts scanned and legacy land records into structured information, compares extracted information with authorized reference records, identifies discrepancies and links verified land records with their corresponding GIS parcels.

---

## Problem

Land records in India exist across different formats, languages and systems.

Legacy records may contain:

- Scanned documents
- Poor-quality images
- Faded or damaged pages
- Printed text
- Handwritten information
- Regional languages
- Inconsistent document formats
- Different representations of survey, khasra and khata information

Manual digitization and verification can be time-consuming and error-prone.

IntelliLandAI aims to provide an intelligent workflow that assists authorized officers in digitizing, validating and geographically linking land records.
